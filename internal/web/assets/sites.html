How proving it works

Put this link anywhere on your site's home page:

<link rel="me" href="{{.ProfileURL}}">

Then press Verify. We read your page and look for that link. Your profile here already carries a rel="me" pointing back at your domain, so the two ends confirm each other. Neither side alone is enough — that is the whole point.

While we are there we also read your h-card for a name and a short note, and any feed you advertise. Nothing is required; whatever is missing just stays empty.
